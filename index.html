About KRAC

Building capacity, transforming communities, and leading agricultural innovation in Kenya.

Kenya Rural Agribusiness College

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern agribusiness, KRAC has grown to become a leading institution in agricultural education in the Mt Kenya region, based in Othaya, Nyeri.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and strong industry partnerships ensure our graduates are well-equipped to lead Kenya's agricultural transformation.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in agribusiness training, innovation, and sustainable rural development in Africa.

Mission

To empower individuals and communities through practical, market-driven agribusiness education and innovation for improved livelihoods and food security.

Our Goals

  • Quality Training — hands-on crop and livestock production.
  • Skills Development — entrepreneurial skills for self-employment.
  • Agribusiness Promotion — value addition and market access.
  • Innovation & Technology — digital agriculture integration.
  • Sustainable Agriculture — climate-smart farming practices.
  • Industry Linkages — partnerships with NGOs and companies.
  • Community Development — outreach and extension services.

Our Impact

  • Economic Impact — increased income through agribusiness.
  • Food Security — reduced post-harvest losses.
  • Skills & Knowledge — practical agricultural capacity.
  • Youth & Women Empowerment — increased participation.
  • Community Development — strengthened local economies.
  • Environmental Impact — climate-smart practices adopted.
  • Institutional Impact — reputable training center established.

Entry Requirements

  • Identity Documents
    Original National ID or Passport
  • Medical Fitness
    Certificate from a recognized medical facility
  • Recommendation
    Letter from a chief, pastor, or school principal
  • Application Fee
    Non-refundable fee of KES 1,000
