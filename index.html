About KRAC

Building capacity, transforming communities, and leading agricultural innovation in Kenya.

Kenya Rural Agribusiness College

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern agribusiness, KRAC has grown to become a leading institution in agricultural education in the Mt Kenya region, based in Othaya, Nyeri.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and strong industry partnerships ensure our graduates are well-equipped to lead Kenya's agricultural transformation.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in agribusiness training, innovation, and sustainable rural development in Africa.

Mission

To empower individuals and communities through practical, market-driven agribusiness education, skills development, and innovation for improved livelihoods and food security.

Our Goals

  • Quality Training — Competency-based, hands-on training in crop and livestock production.
  • Skills Development — Practical and entrepreneurial skills for self-employment.
  • Agribusiness Promotion — Value addition, agro-processing, and market access.
  • Innovation & Technology — Digital agriculture and smart farming integration.
  • Sustainable Agriculture — Climate-smart and environmentally sustainable practices.
  • Industry Linkages — Partnerships with agribusiness companies and NGOs.
  • Community Development — Outreach programs and extension services.

Our Impact

  • Economic Impact — Increased income through agribusiness ventures.
  • Food Security — Increased production and reduced post-harvest losses.
  • Skills & Knowledge — Practical agricultural and entrepreneurial capacity.
  • Youth & Women Empowerment — Increased participation in agribusiness.
  • Community Development — Strengthened local economies.
  • Environmental Impact — Climate-smart farming practices.
  • Institutional Impact — Reputable agribusiness training center.

Admissions

Begin your journey. Admissions Open — Intake Ongoing.

Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Certificate
    Minimum grade of C- (Minus) or equivalent
  • Identity Documents
    Original National ID or Passport
  • Medical Fitness
    Certificate from a recognized medical facility
  • Recommendation
    Letter from a chief, pastor, or school principal
  • Application Fee
    Non-refundable fee of KES 1,000
