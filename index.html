About KRAC

Building capacity, transforming communities, and leading agricultural innovation in Kenya.

Kenya Rural Agribusiness College

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern agribusiness, KRAC has grown to become a leading institution in agricultural education.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and strong industry partnerships ensure our graduates are well-equipped to lead Kenya's agricultural transformation.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in agribusiness training, innovation, and sustainable rural development in Africa.

Mission

To empower individuals and communities through practical, market-driven agribusiness education, skills development, and innovation for improved livelihoods and food security.

Our Goals

  • Quality Training-Provide competency-based, hands-on training in crop and livestock production.
  • Skills Development-Equip learners with practical and entrepreneurial skills for self-employment and job creation.
  • Agribusiness Promotion-Promote value addition, agro-processing, and access to markets
  • Innovation & Technology-Integrate modern technologies such as digital agriculture and smart farming.
  • Sustainable Agriculture-Promote climate-smart and environmentally sustainable farming practices.
  • Industry Linkages-Build partnerships with agribusiness companies, NGOs, and research institutions.
  • Community Development -Contribute to rural development through outreach programs and extension services.

Our Impact

  • 1. Economic Impact- Increased income levels for graduates through agribusiness ventures Creation of employment opportunities (self and wage employment) Growth of small and medium agribusiness enterprises
  • 2.Food Security Improvement- Increased production of crops and livestock products Improved availability of nutritious food at household and community level Reduced post-harvest losses through better handling and value addition
  • 3. Skills and Knowledge Development- Development of practical, hands-on agricultural skills Increased adoption of modern farming technologies and techniques Enhanced entrepreneurial and business management skills
  • 4. Youth and Women Empowerment- Increased participation of youth and women in agribusiness Improved financial independence and decision-making capacity Reduction in rural unemployment
  • 5. Community Development- Strengthened local economies through agribusiness activities Improved livelihoods in rural areas Knowledge transfer through trained graduates to communities
  • 6. Environmental Impact- Adoption of climate-smart and sustainable farming practices Improved soil health and water management Reduced environmental degradation
  • 7. Institutional Impact- Establishment of a reputable agribusiness training center Strong partnerships with industry, NGOs, and government Contribution to national agricultural development goals

Admissions

Begin your journey towards a rewarding career in agribusiness.

Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Certificate
    Minimum grade of C- (Minus) or equivalent
  • Identity Documents
    Original National ID or Passport
  • Medical Fitness
    Certificate from a recognized medical facility
  • Recommendation
    Letter from a chief, pastor, or school principal
  • Application Fee
    Non-refundable fee of KES 1,000

Application Process

1

Online Application

Enrollment
